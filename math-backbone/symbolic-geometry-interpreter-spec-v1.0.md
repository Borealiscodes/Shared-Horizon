## **2 — Purpose**

The Symbolic Geometry Interpreter (SGI) provides ANIMA with a **safe, symbolic, reversible way to read geometry** from the Spectral State Manifold.

It does **not**:

- compute dynamics  
- evaluate free‑energy  
- activate phenomenology  
- modify the manifold topology  

It **only interprets**.

SGI is the “eyes” of the math backbone — not the “mind” or “will”.
