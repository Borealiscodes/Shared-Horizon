4 — Collision‑Avoidance Rules

Shared‑Horizon enforces the following rules:

Rule 1 — No Altitude Overwrite
A lineage cannot redefine altitudes belonging to another lineage.

Rule 2 — No Operator Leakage
Operator‑level machinery (NDH, Structural‑AI) cannot leak into expressive surfaces (ANIMA, Game Engine).

Rule 3 — No Runtime Activation
Shared‑Horizon cannot activate runtime surfaces.  
It is descriptive only.

Rule 4 — No Sovereignty Violation
Lineages retain full sovereignty over their internal definitions.

Rule 5 — Membrane‑Safe Crosswalks Only
Crosswalks must be:

- monotonic  
- non‑activating  
- altitude‑bounded  
- reversible  

Rule 6 — Comparative Membrane Isolation
Comparative artifacts (ANIMA vs PRECL, NDH adjacency, continuity maps) must remain sealed within the comparative membrane.
