- discontinuous  
- prismatic

- spectrally unstable

- a crease in fabric

But in **higher‑dimensional geometry**.





Hyperfolds are **non‑Euclidean** because:

- parallel lines do not stay parallel  
- geodesics diverge or converge unpredictably  
- curvature changes abruptly  
- adjacency becomes multi‑layered  
- spectral signatures split or merge  

They are the first true discontinuities in your curvature ladder.
