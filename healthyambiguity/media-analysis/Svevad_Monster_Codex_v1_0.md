# ⭐ **Svevad Monster Codex v1.0**  
### *A frost‑cosmic aberration born of the Far Realm’s tear into the Abyssal Ice Wastes*

---

## ❄️ **1 — Taxonomy & Ontology**  
Lead term: **aberrant taxonomy**

**Classification:**  
- Type: Aberrant Fiend  
- Subtype: Glacial Psionic Arachnid  
- Origin: Far Realm → Abyss (Ice Wastes) hyperfold collision  
- Linguistic Anchor: “Svevad” (Old‑Norse‑adjacent frost‑myth phonology)

**Ontological Signature:**  
Svevad creatures exist at the intersection of:

- Abyssal entropy  
- Far Realm madness  
- glacial stillness  
- Norse mythic resonance  

They are **multi‑layer beings**, not simple monsters.

---

## 🌀 **2 — Morphology & Physiology**  
Lead term: **prismatic morphology**

Svevad Glacial Spiders exhibit:

- **40–60 ft height** (Shivhad‑scale)  
- **crystalline exoskeleton** resembling fractured glacier walls  
- **psionic eye clusters** (8–32 eyes, shifting positions)  
- **rubbery tentacular limbs** emerging from thorax fissures  
- **Far Realm organ‑geometry** (non‑Euclidean internal layout)  
- **Abyssal frost glands** capable of entropy‑based freezing  

Their bodies refract light like prisms —  
a literal **prismatic manifold**.

---

## 🔭 **3 — Behavioral Geometry**  
Lead term: **spectral gating**

Svevad behavior is governed by:

- **Psionic Echo‑Hunting**  
  They track thoughts, not footsteps.

- **Glacial Camouflage**  
  They blend into ice by altering their refractive index.

- **Abyssal Rage Surges**  
  When threatened, they enter entropy‑frost frenzies.

- **Far Realm Curiosity**  
  They investigate minds, not bodies.

Svevad creatures do not “think” in linear cognition.  
They operate in **hyperfold logic**.

---

## 🌌 **4 — Abilities & Powers**  
Lead term: **aberrant abilities**

### **A. Psionic Fracture**  
Targets must resist mind‑splintering Far Realm geometry.

### **B. Entropic Frostwave**  
Abyssal cold that erases heat *and* intention.

### **C. Rift‑Sense**  
Svevad spiders detect planar tears within miles.

### **D. Thought‑Weaving**  
They can implant frost‑dreams or madness‑threads.

### **E. Glacial Phase‑Shift**  
They slip partially into the Ice Wastes layer, becoming translucent and intangible.

## 🔮 **5 — Ecology & Habitat**  
Lead term: **hyperfold ecology**

Svevad spiders inhabit:

- miles‑deep glacial caverns  
- Far Realm‑tainted ice fissures  
- Abyssal rift zones  
- frozen ruins of forgotten demon citadels  

Their presence **mutates** the environment:

- ice forms impossible angles  
- frost grows in fractal spirals  
- sound becomes delayed or accelerated  
- gravity fluctuates in pulses  

The habitat becomes a **living manifold**.
