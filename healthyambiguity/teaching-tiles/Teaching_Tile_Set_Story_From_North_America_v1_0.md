# 🧭 **Epistemic Divergence Map**  
### *How each lens interprets the same scene differently*

| Perspective | Epistemic Mode | Divergence |
|------------|----------------|------------|
| **Bill Nye** | Pop‑science clarity | Frames the moment as a simple experiment in compassion. |
| **Tyson** | Cosmic scale | Reinterprets the spider as a statistically tiny but cosmically meaningful being. |
| **Mary Oliver** | Poetic attention | Turns the spider into a small sacred moment of noticing. |
| **Le Guin** | Anthropological storytelling | Reads the father’s choice as a political act of gentle power. |
| **Sagan** | Cosmic awe | Connects the moral lesson to universal origins and starstuff. |
| **Nietzsche** | Existential strength | Sees the father’s refusal as an act of self‑overcoming and moral independence. |

### 🌀 Divergence Summary  
- **Science lenses** (Nye, Tyson) emphasize *curiosity* and *scale*.  
- **Poetic lenses** (Oliver, Le Guin) emphasize *attention* and *power*.  
- **Cosmic lenses** (Sagan) emphasize *origin* and *interconnection*.  
- **Existential lenses** (Nietzsche) emphasize *strength* and *self‑creation*.  

Together they form a **prismatic manifold of interpretation** — each bending the same moral hyperfold in a different chromatic direction.
