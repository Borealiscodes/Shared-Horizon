## 🍎 **What apples represent in myth (the core meanings)**  
Across cultures, apples symbolize **knowledge, temptation, immortality, discord, abundance, and the boundary between mortal and divine**.  
This isn’t speculation — it’s consistent across multiple myth traditions.   [symbolhubs.com](https://symbolhubs.com/symbol/apple-symbol/)

---

## 🍏 **Major mythic meanings, with provenance**

### **1. Temptation & Forbidden Knowledge (Christian tradition)**  
The apple becomes the symbol of the *Tree of Knowledge* — not because Genesis names it, but because medieval Latin blurred “malus” (evil) and “malus” (apple).  
Thus the apple becomes the icon of **temptation, loss of innocence, and the fall**.   [Europeana](https://www.europeana.eu/en/stories/how-the-apple-became-an-important-symbol-in-culture-science-and-story)

### **2. Immortality (Greek, Norse, Celtic)**  
Golden apples appear repeatedly as **gifts of eternal youth**:

- **Greek:** Gaia gives Hera golden apples promising immortality; Heracles must steal them.   [alimentarium](https://www.alimentarium.org/en/fact-sheet/apple-discord-and-beauty)  
- **Norse:** Iðunn guards apples that keep the gods young; when she’s taken, the gods begin to age.   [alimentarium](https://www.alimentarium.org/en/fact-sheet/apple-discord-and-beauty)  
- **Celtic:** Lugh is depicted holding apples symbolizing **immortality, power, prosperity**.   [alimentarium](https://www.alimentarium.org/en/fact-sheet/apple-discord-and-beauty)

### **3. Discord & Catastrophe (Greek)**  
Eris throws the golden apple marked **“to the fairest”**, triggering the Judgement of Paris and ultimately the **Trojan War**.  
The apple becomes a symbol of **conflict born from desire**.   [alimentarium](https://www.alimentarium.org/en/fact-sheet/apple-discord-and-beauty)

### **4. Desire, Seduction, and Beauty (Western folklore)**  
Apples appear in fairy tales like **Snow White**, where the fruit is irresistible and deadly — reinforcing the apple as a symbol of **seductive danger**.   [Europeana](https://www.europeana.eu/en/stories/how-the-apple-became-an-important-symbol-in-culture-science-and-story)

### **5. Abundance & Prosperity (Celtic, Norse)**  
In some traditions, apples are simply **gifts of plenty**, representing harvest, nourishment, and divine favor.   [symbolhubs.com](https://symbolhubs.com/symbol/apple-symbol/)

## 🍎 **The unifying theme**  
Across myths, apples are **never neutral**.  
They are always:

- a **test**,  
- a **gift**,  
- a **trap**,  
- or a **doorway** to something bigger than the person who touches them.

They mark the moment when a character crosses a threshold — moral, divine, emotional, or existential.
