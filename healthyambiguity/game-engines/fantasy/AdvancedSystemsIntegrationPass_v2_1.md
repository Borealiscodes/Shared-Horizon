The emotional update operator is defined as:

\[
U_{\text{emotional}} = \Delta_{\text{rasa}} + \Delta_{\text{drift}} + \Delta_{\text{continuity}} + \Delta_{\text{resonance}} + \Delta_{\text{inversion}}

### Components:

- **Δ_resonance** — seasonal + environmental amplification  
- **Δ_inversion** — corruption‑driven emotional distortion  

This operator is applied to:

- corruption  
- seasons  
- world‑state

Each advanced subsystem must bind to rasa physics using the following rules:

## **Rule 1 — Read Rasa First**

- primary rasa  
- secondary rasa  
- drift threshold  
- continuity field  
- resonance modifiers  

This ensures emotional context is always known.

---

## **Rule 2 — Apply Mechanical Update**  
Subsystem performs its normal update:

- zone difficulty  
- faction power  
- settlement growth  
- monster pressure  
- magic potency  
- loot distribution  

---

## **Rule 3 — Apply Emotional Update**  
Subsystem applies emotional deltas:

- rasa shifts  
- drift accumulation  
- continuity changes  
- resonance effects  
- inversion effects  

---

## **Rule 4 — Write Back to W(t)**  
Subsystem writes:

- updated rasa state  
- updated drift  
- updated continuity  
- updated resonance  
- updated inversion  

into the world‑state.
