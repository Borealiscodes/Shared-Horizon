# 🧬 **Backstory (IC‑Ready)**

### **Childhood — Willowfen’s Quiet Daughter**  
Lira grew up in a village where memory lanterns were tended like gardens.  
Her mother taught her that every lantern holds a truth someone was afraid to speak.

### **Defining Joy — The Lantern of First Light**  
At age 12, she lit her first lantern.  
It glowed rose‑gold — a rare śṛṅgāra signature — marking her as a future keeper.

### **Defining Trauma — The Night of the Broken Lantern**  
A corrupted creature shattered a lantern she was tending.  
The memory inside spilled into her mind:  
a scream, a betrayal, a face she didn’t recognize.

She has never told anyone what she saw.

### **Relationships**
- **Mother:** Missing since the Lantern Shattering  
- **Mentor:** Elder Rowan, Rangers Guild  
- **Rival:** A fellow keeper who suspects she’s hiding something  
- **Unknown:** The face from the broken lantern

### **Unresolved Emotional Arc**
She carries a truth she didn’t choose — and the world knows it.
