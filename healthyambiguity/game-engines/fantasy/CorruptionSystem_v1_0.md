# 🧠 **2. Corruption Types**

Each corruption type is a Guided Link so you can explore or extend it.

- **Inversion Corruption** — rasa flips into its opposite  
- **Fragmentation Corruption** — rasa splits into unstable shards  
- **Overload Corruption** — rasa exceeds safe thresholds  
- **Bleed Corruption** — corruption spreads across zones  
- **Dormant Corruption** — corruption sleeps until triggered  

Each type has unique emotional and mechanical effects.

# 🔮 **3. Emotional Physics of Corruption**

Corruption modifies rasa using three operations:

### **Rasa Inversion**
\[
\text{invert}(r) = r_{\text{opposite}}
\]

Examples:
- śānta → bhayānaka  
- śṛṅgāra → bībhatsa  
- vīra → raudra  

### **Rasa Fragmentation**
\[
\text{fragment}(r) = r_1 + r_2 + r_3
\]

Example:
- vīra → raudra + bībhatsa + bhayānaka  

### **Rasa Overload**
\[
\text{overload} = \text{depth} + \text{motion} > \theta_{\text{corruption}}
\]

Overload causes emotional instability.

# 🧬 **4. Corruption Propagation Model**

Corruption spreads using:

\[
C(t+1) = C(t) + \Delta_{\text{events}} + \Delta_{\text{season}} + \Delta_{\text{zone}} + \Delta_{\text{magic}}
\]

Propagation vectors:

- **events** (especially raudra, bhayānaka, bībhatsa)  
- **seasons** (autumn accelerates, winter mutates)  
- **zones** (high difficulty zones amplify)  
- **magic traditions** (void, curse, decay schools)

Corruption destabilizes factions:

- morale collapse  
- leadership inversion  
- schisms  
- coups  
- corrupted cult formation  
- diplomatic breakdown  

Factions with **bhayānaka** or **raudra** resonance are more vulnerable.

# 🏙️ **7. Corruption Effects on Settlements**

Corruption fractures settlements:

- population decline  
- fear-driven migrations  
- resource scarcity  
- corruption outbreaks  
- settlement collapse events  

Settlements with low continuity fields collapse faster.

# 🔮 **8. Corruption Effects on Magic Traditions**

Magic traditions distort under corruption:

- spell inversion  
- mana-flow instability  
- corrupted spell variants  
- tradition schisms  
- ritual failures  
- arcane contamination  

Void, shadow, and decay traditions are highly reactive.

Seasons modify corruption:

- inversion risk increases  

### Winter  
- corruption mutates (+10%)  
- subtle inversion storms
