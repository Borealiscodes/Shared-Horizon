# ❄️ **Seasonal Cycle Model (Fantasy Engine v1.0)**  
*Global Dynamics Layer • Emotional Resonance • Environmental Physics*

## 1. 🎯 Purpose  
The Seasonal Cycle Model defines how **seasons influence the world**, both mechanically and emotionally.  
It integrates:

- environmental physics  
- rasa resonance  
- event frequency  
- corruption behavior  
- monster migrations  
- faction resource cycles  
- settlement mood  
- magic tradition potency  

Seasons become **world‑shaping forces**, not just background flavor.

---

# 2. 🌈 The Four Seasons as Emotional-Environmental States

Each season has:

- **environmental profile**  
- **rasa resonance signature**  
- **mechanical modifiers**  
- **event biases**  
- **corruption behavior shifts**  
- **magic tradition flux**  
- **monster migration patterns**  
- **settlement mood changes**  

---

# 3. 🌱 **Spring — Renewal Cycle**

### Environmental Profile
- warming climate  
- increased rainfall  
- plant growth  
- creature emergence  

### Rasa Resonance
- **śṛṅgāra** (tenderness, beauty)  
- **hāsya** (joy, playfulness)

### Mechanical Effects
- settlement growth +10%  
- monster spawn rate +8%  
- faction diplomacy +5%  

### Event Bias
- festivals  
- migrations  
- fertility rituals  

### Corruption Behavior
- corruption slows (−12%)  
- inversion resistance increases  

### Magic Tradition Flux
- nature, spirit, and healing magic strengthened  

---

# 4. ☀️ **Summer — Expansion Cycle**

### Environmental Profile
- heat  
- storms  
- high energy  

### Rasa Resonance
- **vīra** (courage, resolve)  
- **adbhuta** (wonder, awe)

### Mechanical Effects
- faction conflict +15%  
- monster aggression +12%  
- magic potency +10%  

### Event Bias
- wars  
- heroic quests  
- celestial alignments  

### Corruption Behavior
- corruption stabilizes (0%)  
- bleed zones remain constant  

### Magic Tradition Flux
- fire, storm, celestial magic strengthened  

---

# 5. 🍂 **Autumn — Decline Cycle**

### Environmental Profile
- cooling  
- decay  
- harvest  

### Rasa Resonance
- **karuṇā** (sorrow, compassion)  
- **bhayānaka** (fear, dread)

### Mechanical Effects
- settlement productivity −8%  
- faction morale −10%  
- monster migrations +14%  

### Event Bias
- famines  
- refugee influx  
- fear-driven faction shifts  

### Corruption Behavior
- corruption accelerates (+18%)  
- inversion risk increases  

### Magic Tradition Flux
- curse, shadow, and decay magic strengthened  

---

# 6. ❄️ **Winter — Stillness Cycle**

### Environmental Profile
- cold  
- scarcity  
- low mobility  

### Rasa Resonance
- **śānta** (stillness, equanimity)  
- **bībhatsa** (disgust, revulsion)

### Mechanical Effects
- monster activity −20%  
- settlement growth −15%  
- faction conflict −12%  

### Event Bias
- hibernation  
- spiritual rituals  
- defensive wars  

### Corruption Behavior
- corruption mutates (+10%)  
- inversion becomes subtle  

### Magic Tradition Flux
- ice, void, and stillness magic strengthened

# 8. 🧩 Seasonal Drift & Continuity

Seasons modify emotional physics:

### Drift Accumulation
\[
\text{drift}(t+1) = \text{drift}(t) + R_{\text{season}}
\]

### Continuity Field
\[
C(t+1) = C(t) - \Delta_{\text{seasonal_instability}}
\]

Winter stabilizes continuity.  
Autumn destabilizes it.
