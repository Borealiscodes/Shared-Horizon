# 🧮 **2 — Core Math Substrate**  
Lead term: **labyrinth physics**

### **2.1 Spatial Distortion**

Effective distance inside the labyrinth:

\[
d_{\text{eff}} = d \cdot \left(1 + k \cdot e^{-\frac{r}{R_{\text{lab}}}}\right)
\]

- \(d\): map distance  
- \(r\): distance from center  
- \(k\): curvature intensity  
- \(R_{\text{lab}}\): labyrinth radius  

---

### **2.2 Despair Field**

Each PC has Despair \(D_i\), Hope \(H_i\), Resolve \(R_i\).

\[
\Delta D_i = \alpha \cdot k \cdot \left(1 - \frac{H_i + R_i}{H_{\max} + R_{\max}}\right)
\]

Break threshold:

\[
D_i \ge D_{\text{break}} \Rightarrow \text{Labyrinth Break}
\]

---

### **2.3 Hope Counterfield**

Hope actions grant:

\[
\Delta H_i = \beta \cdot A
\]

Team Hope:

\[
H_{\text{team}} = \sum_i H_i
\]

Curvature dampening:

\[
k_{\text{eff}} = \frac{k}{1 + \gamma \cdot \frac{H_{\text{team}}}{H_{\max,\text{team}}}}
\]

---

### **2.4 Core Stability**

\[
S_{\text{next}} = S - \lambda \cdot H_{\text{team}} + \mu \cdot \sum_i D_i
\]

Outcomes:

- \(S_{\text{next}} \le 0\): **Collapse**  
- \(S_{\text{next}} \ge S_{\text{ascend}}\): **Ascension**

# 🎮 **3 — Player Sheet (PC Stats)**  
Lead term: **character creation**

Each PC has:

- **Resolve (R):** resists despair  
- **Empathy (E):** generates hope  
- **Focus (F):** navigates distortion  
- **Hope (H):** dynamic emotional buffer  
- **Despair (D):** dynamic emotional gravity  

Starting values:

- \(R, E, F\) between 1–5  
- \(H = E\)  
- \(D = 0\)

# 🌀 **5 — Turn Structure**

### **5.1 Exploration Phase**

- GM describes distorted space using \(d_{\text{eff}}\).  
- PCs make **Focus checks** to avoid drift.

### **5.2 Emotional Phase**

Each round:

\[
D_i \leftarrow D_i + \Delta D_i
\]

PCs may perform **Hope actions**.

### **5.3 Confrontation Phase**

At the core:

\[
S \leftarrow S_{\text{next}}
\]

Check for **Collapse** or **Ascension**.

# ❄️ **6 — Sample Adventure: “The Frozen Choir of Svevad”**  
Lead term: **sample adventure**

### **Premise**

A glacial city built over a forgotten rift manifests a Witch‑Labyrinth called **The Frozen Choir**, formed from collective grief.

### **Labyrinth Stats**

- \(k = 2.5\)  
- \(R_{\text{lab}} = 500\) m  
- \(\alpha = 0.6\), \(\beta = 1.0\), \(\gamma = 1.0\)  
- \(S = 40\), \(S_{\text{ascend}} = 80\)  
- \(D_{\text{break}} = 10\)

### **Key Scenes**

1. **The Ice‑Echo Gate**  
   PCs enter and immediately experience doubled distances.

2. **The Choir Corridor**  
   Voices of lost children create despair pulses.

3. **The Frost‑Memory Chamber**  
   PCs perform Hope actions tied to personal memories.

4. **The Core: The Silent Singer**  
   A frozen figure whose grief powers the labyrinth.

### **Outcome**

- Collapse → release the Frozen Choir  
- Ascension → catastrophic frost‑psionic event
