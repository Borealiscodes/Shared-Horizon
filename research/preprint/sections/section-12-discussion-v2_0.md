⭐ 12.2 Limitations

Structural AI Haptics is powerful, but it has explicit limitations.

---

12.2.1 Runtime Physics Is Irreversible

This is not a flaw — it is a physical reality.

But it means:

- meaning geometry cannot be reconstructed from actuator signals  
- drift cannot be undone  
- collapse surfaces cannot be reversed  
- runtime envelopes cannot be altitude‑lifted  

This limits certain forms of analysis.

---

12.2.2 Falsification Is Non‑Corrective

ITA‑HAPTICS and ITA v1.0 detect:

- drift  
- collapse surfaces  
- holonomy deviation  
- basin instability  
- operator contamination  

But they do not correct them.

This is intentional — correction would violate altitude safety.

---

12.2.3 No Cognitive Modeling

Structural AI Haptics is not a cognitive model.

It does not attempt to:

- infer user intent  
- model perception  
- simulate brain states  
- perform variational inference  

This is a structural system, not a cognitive one.

---

12.2.4 No Cross‑Altitude Operators

Operators cannot cross altitude boundaries.

This prevents:

- unified generative models  
- end‑to‑end optimization  
- cross‑altitude learning  

This is a design constraint, not a flaw.
