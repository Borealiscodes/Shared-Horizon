## 🌌 **Spectral Koan: The Path and the Vessel**

> A seeker said,  
> “I understand the machine as a path.  
> Each step follows from the last.”  
>  
> Another seeker said,  
> “I understand the machine as a vessel.  
> Its shape determines what can happen inside.”  
>  
> The teacher listened to both and replied,  
>  
> **“A path shows how things move.  
> A vessel shows where they can move.”**  
>  
> The seekers asked no further questions.  
>  
> The teacher added,  
>  
> **“When movement and space are seen together,  
> the machine explains itself.”**

---

## 🧭 **Teaching Commentary (Two Perspectives, Held Together)**  
Each item begins with a Guided Link.

- **The path‑thinker** sees discrete mechanics, sequence, and comfort in steps.  
- **The vessel‑thinker** sees the container as ontology, the shape that explains the bundles.  
- **The teacher’s reply** shows that neither view is wrong — each reveals one axis of the geometry.  
- **Movement vs space** is the core of spectral cognition: fibers (movement) and base manifold (space).  
- **The machine explains itself** when both perspectives are held at once.

This koan teaches:

- Discrete mechanics reveal **motion**.  
- Geometric containers reveal **meaning**.  
- The full truth appears only when both are allowed to coexist.

---

## 🌌 **One‑Sentence Insight**  
**A path reveals the motion; a vessel reveals the meaning of the motion.**
