# ⚠️ **Altitude Equivalence Disclaimer (Integrated)**  
### *NDH Altitudes Are Varied; Crosswalk Shows Relationships, Not Totality*

NDH altitudes span multiple governance, mechanical, and sealed‑layer functions.  
Not all NDH altitude bands have meaningful equivalents in EcoGrid, Shared‑Horizon, or Spectral‑Geometry cognition.

In particular:

- **RP‑Altitude** is a **reflective/public documentation envelope**, not a geometric, mechanical, or cognitive layer.  
  It is **non‑activating**, **non‑computational**, and **non‑mechanical**, and therefore does **not** map cleanly to any other altitude system.

Because of this, the Cross‑Altitude Mapping is:

- a **relationship map**, not a **total equivalence map**  
- a **translation surface**, not a **unification**  
- a **contextual alignment**, not a **collapse** of altitude systems  

It shows **how altitudes relate**, not **how they match**.

This protects:

- sealed NDH layers  
- expressive EcoGrid surfaces  
- epistemic Shared‑Horizon scaffolds  
- cognitive spectral‑geometry layers  

and prevents altitude drift or unsafe abstraction.
