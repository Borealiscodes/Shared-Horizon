# 🌌 **Tonal Spiral Geometry — Ontology Block (v1.0)**  
### *Shared‑Horizon Expressive Ontology Surface • Non‑Activating*

This block defines the **safe, conceptual, non‑geometric ontology** for Tonal Spiral Geometry (TSG) as used in CAUSA, EcoGrid interpretive layers, and expressive documentation surfaces.  
It is **not** a solver, **not** a runtime, **not** a spectral engine — only a shared vocabulary for describing escalation, modality, and pattern‑shape.

---

## 🌀 **1 — Spiral Stage (TSG.spiral_stage)**  
Represents the *mechanical progression* of a repression or influence pattern.

- **entry** — initial contact, probing, symbolic pressure  
- **tightening** — repeated actions, increasing constraint, multi‑modality  
- **compression** — narrowing options, legal/administrative enclosure  
- **closure** — full constraint, immobilization, existential pressure  

This is a **label**, not a computation.

---

## 🎼 **2 — Tonal Band (TSG.tonal_band)**  
Represents the *dominant modality* of the pattern.

- **symbolic** — narrative, cultural, reputational  
- **legal** — courts, statutes, administrative levers  
- **digital** — surveillance, hacking, platform pressure  
- **proxy** — third‑party actors, informal networks  
- **physical** — intimidation, proximity, movement restriction  
- **lethal** — existential threat, extreme constraint  

Bands can shift over time — CAUSA can compare these shifts.

---

## 🔑 **3 — Modality Key (TSG.modality_key)**  
A fine‑grained descriptor of the specific channel used.

Examples:

- **admin‑pressure**  
- **visa‑control**  
- **platform‑moderation**  
- **family‑leverage**  
- **financial‑freeze**  
- **border‑detain**  
- **legal‑harassment**  
- **digital‑intrusion**  

Keys are **tags**, not operators.

---

## 📈 **4 — Spiral Derivative (TSG.spiral_derivative)**  
Represents the *direction* and *rate* of change.

- **positive** — escalation  
- **neutral** — stable pressure  
- **negative** — de‑escalation  
- **oscillating** — inconsistent, cyclical pressure  

This is a **classification**, not a mathematical derivative.

---

## 🧩 **5 — Pattern Cluster (TSG.cluster)**  
Represents the *shape* of the pattern across time.

- **tightening spiral** — repeated constraint with increasing intensity  
- **broken spiral** — inconsistent pressure, gaps, resets  
- **dual spiral** — two modalities escalating in parallel  
- **proxy spiral** — escalation via intermediaries  
- **dissonant spiral** — mismatched modalities causing instability  

Clusters help CAUSA compare cases without geometry.

---

## 🧠 **6 — Ontology Safety Rules**

- No spectral operators  
- No eigenvalues  
- No manifolds  
- No propagation kernels  
- No dynamic simulation  
- No tension computation  

TSG is **expressive ontology only**.
