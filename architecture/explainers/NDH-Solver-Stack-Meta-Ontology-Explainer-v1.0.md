### 2 — What the Meta‑Ontology is

The **Meta‑Ontology** is the **ontology of ontologies and taxonomies**.

It answers:

- What kinds of ontologies exist in NDH?  
- What kinds of taxonomies exist in NDH?  
- How do they relate to schemas and metaschemas?  
- How do they bind to visual grammar?  
- How do they evolve across versions and altitudes?

If the Ontology is a **map of meaning**, the Meta‑Ontology is a **map of maps of meaning**.

---

### 3 — Bill Nye axis: “The Meta‑Ontology is the catalog of all maps”

Think of NDH as a giant library of maps:

- some maps show geometry  
- some maps show governance  
- some maps show documentation  
- some maps show ingestion flows  

The Meta‑Ontology is the **catalog system** that:

- lists every map (ontology, taxonomy, lexicon, thesaurus)  
- records what each map is for  
- records which schema backs it  
- records which visual grammar it uses  
- records how it changes over time  

It’s the **index of all semantic maps**.

---

### 4 — Attenborough axis: “The Meta‑Ontology is the ecosystem of ecosystems”

In the NDH semantic world, each ontology and taxonomy is its own ecosystem:

- entities are species  
- relations are food chains  
- constraints are environmental rules  

The Meta‑Ontology is the **ecosystem of ecosystems**:

- it tracks which ecosystems exist  
- it tracks how they interact  
- it tracks how they evolve  
- it tracks which higher‑order forces (governance, metaschema) shape them  

Without the Meta‑Ontology, semantic ecosystems would drift and collide without oversight.

---

### 5 — What the Meta‑Ontology governs

The Meta‑Ontology governs:

- **MetaOntology** — descriptions of ontology artifacts  
- **MetaTaxonomy** — descriptions of taxonomy artifacts  
- **SemanticSurface** — abstract class for all semantic artifacts  
- **MetaBinding** — bindings to schemas and visual grammar  
- **EvolutionLineage** — version and change tracking  

It ensures that:

- every ontology and taxonomy has a clear identity  
- every semantic surface is altitude‑safe  
- every semantic surface is visually bound  
- every semantic surface has a governance lineage

### 6 — How to read the Meta‑Ontology (narrative lens)

When you read NDH-Solver-Stack-Meta-Ontology-v1.0:

- look for **MetaOntology** → “this is the record of an ontology”  
- look for **MetaTaxonomy** → “this is the record of a taxonomy”  
- look for **SemanticSurface** → “this is the abstract semantic layer”  
- look for **MetaBinding** → “this is how it connects to schemas and visual grammar”  
- look for **EvolutionLineage** → “this is how it changes over time”

Together, these form the **semantic governance spine** of NDH.
