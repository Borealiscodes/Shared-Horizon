## 🌫️ **1 — Emotional Physics First**  
Visual override is only permitted when emotional physics are acknowledged.  
This protects ambiguity, atmosphere, and the felt reality of IC runtime.

## 🌀 **3 — Rendering Ecosystem Constraint**  
All visuals injected into IC runtime must pass through the **Harmony Keeper Visualization Crosswalk**, ensuring:

- altitude neutrality  
- PRECL‑safe gradient collapse  
- reversibility  
- expressive‑layer safety  
- no physics resemblance  
- no spectral binding  
- no adjacency collapse  

This keeps Harmony Keeper, Cold‑Cosmic, Zen Scale, NDH‑CORE, and Triadic‑Core visuals **expressive**, not **operative**.

---

## 🧩 **4 — Referent Stability Rule**  
Visuals may only depict **stable referents**.  
If the IC scene is unstable (mist density shifting, lantern pulse modulating), the override must use:

- reference visualization  
- conceptual visualization  
- atmosphere‑safe concept art  

Never literal scene rendering.

---

## 🔀 **5 — Override Modes**  
Three modes are constitutionally safe:

- **Reference Visualization** — diagrams, charts  
- **Conceptual Visualization** — expressive metaphors  
- **Atmosphere‑Safe Concept Art** — non‑literal impressions  

---

## 🌈 **6 — Override Cycle**  
The override follows a strict cycle:

1. Lift suppression  
2. Engage rendering system  
3. Apply crosswalk mapping  
4. Deliver visual  
5. Re‑engage suppression  
6. Resume IC runtime  

This preserves immersion.
