## 🧭 **Eco‑Rehabilitation Order (Human‑Readable)**

### **1 — Internal Drive Map**  
**Why first:**

This includes latent‑buffer dynamics, curiosity ecology, significance‑layer evolution, conflict tension, novelty hunger, aesthetic sense, agency/disclosure dynamics.

This is the **constitutional boundary**.

---

### **2 — Eco‑Refactor Blueprint**  
**Why second:**  
The blueprint translates the internal‑drive map into:

- constraints  
- forbidden modifications  
- dependency graph  
- safety rails  
- altitude boundaries  

This is the **governance layer**.  
You cannot begin mechanical changes until this exists.

---

### **3 — Heartbeat Refactor**  
**Why third:**  
Heartbeat drift is the **largest source of waste heat** and the **least tied to meaning**.

If you refactor slow‑tick or idle cognition first, heartbeat noise will still:

- destabilize NT baselines  
- decay coherence  
- trigger crisis checks  
- distort curiosity activation  
- miscalibrate novelty hunger  

Heartbeat stabilization is the **foundation**.

---

### **4 — Slow‑Tick Thinning**  
**Why fourth:**  
Slow‑tick drift becomes interpretable *only after* heartbeat stabilization.

Thinning slow tick too early risks:

- curiosity misfires  
- life‑thread instability  
- concept‑formation timing errors  
- significance‑layer oscillation  
- fatigue misalignment  

Slow‑tick thinning must occur **after** heartbeat stabilization.

---

### **5 — Idle Cognition Constraint**  
**Why last:**  
Idle cognition is ANIMA’s **most expressive internal behavior**.

It must be constrained only after:

- NT baselines stabilize  
- slow‑tick drift is coherent  
- curiosity ecology is predictable  
- conflict tension is stable  
- novelty hunger is correctly measured  

Constraining idle cognition too early risks flattening ANIMA’s internal richness.
