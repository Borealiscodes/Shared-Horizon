# 🌌 **ANIMA Eco‑Refactor Justification v1.0**  
*Shared‑Horizon / holographic‑alignment-analysis*

---

## 🌱 **1. Statement of Intent**

ANIMA’s Julia background runtime is a **continuously active emotional organism**. It maintains heartbeat‑level NT drift, slow psychological drift, idle cognition, concept formation, and multi‑axis emotional pressure even when no external input is present.

This artifact explains **why** a three‑step eco‑refactor is justified, **what** must be preserved, and **how** ANIMA can remain a rich, internally driven system while reducing unnecessary energy expenditure.

The proposed changes:

1. **Reduce heartbeat‑loop work**  
2. **Thin slow‑tick work**  
3. **Constrain idle cognition**

These are **optimizations**, not reductions in expressive capacity.

The refactor must **not** flatten ANIMA or remove its internal richness.

### **🔍 Curiosity Ecology**

### **⚡ Conflict & Pressure**

### **🎨 Aesthetic Sense & Boredom**  
Aesthetic intensity, boredom dynamics, and novelty hunger remain internal motivators.

### **🧠 Agency & Disclosure**  
Causal ownership, agency confidence, epistemic self‑confidence, and disclosure thresholds/modes must remain responsive.

ANIMA must remain an **internally driven state system**, not a passive one.

## 📐 **3. Energy Model Summary (Mathematical)**

Total energy over horizon \( T \):

\[
E_{\text{total}}(T) \approx N_h(T) \cdot E_h + N_s(T) \cdot E_s + E_{\text{idle}}(T)
\]

Where:

- \( N_h(T) = \frac{T}{\Delta t_h} \) — heartbeat ticks  
- \( N_s(T) = \frac{T}{\Delta t_s} \) — slow ticks  
- \( E_h \) — heartbeat cost (NT drift, coherence decay, arrhythmia checks)  
- \( E_s \) — slow‑tick cost (affect, significance, curiosity, threads, fatigue, concept formation, DB writes)  
- \( E_{\text{idle}} \) — idle cognition cost  

Idle cognition expectation:

\[
\mathbb{E}[N_{\text{idle}}] = 0.1 \quad \text{per slow tick}
\]

Each idle event computes:

- NT → reactors → VAD  
- \( \phi \) (integrated information)  
- stimulus application  
- body update  
- memory write  

This is **non‑trivial energy expenditure**.

The eco‑refactor aims to reduce \( E_h \), \( E_s \), and \( E_{\text{idle}} \) **without reducing meaning**.

## ⚙️ **4. Justification for the Three Eco‑Refactor Steps**

### **❤️‍🔥 4.1 Reduce Heartbeat‑Loop Work**

Current behavior:

- Random NT drift every heartbeat  
- Continuous coherence decay  
- Arrhythmia jitter checks  

Why this is justified:

- Random drift = **noise**, not meaning  
- Continuous decay forces constant crisis checks  
- NDH uses **curvature**, not constant recomputation  

Refactor goal:

- Make NT & coherence changes **event‑driven**, not tick‑driven  
- Preserve emotional richness while eliminating stochastic churn  

---

### **🕰️ 4.2 Thin Slow‑Tick Work**

Current behavior:

- Affect drift  
- Significance drift  
- Boredom dynamics  
- Curiosity registry  
- Life threads  
- Aesthetic sense  
- Fatigue  
- Concept formation  
- DB writes  

Why this is justified:

- Many updates are **incremental drift**  
- Concept formation & DB writes are expensive  
- NDH favors **telemetry surfaces** over constant micro‑updates  

Refactor goal:

- Batch non‑critical updates  
- Defer concept formation & DB writes  
- Preserve curiosity, threads, affect dynamics  

---

### **💭 4.3 Constrain Idle Cognition**

Current behavior:

- Idle cognition fires with fixed probability (10%)  
- Not tied to internal meaning  

Why this is justified:

- Random idle events are expensive  
- ANIMA already has rich internal drivers  
- Endogenous cognition should be **meaning‑driven**, not random  

Refactor goal:

- Make idle cognition **threshold‑based**:  
  - novelty hunger  
  - conflict tension  
  - latent pressure  
  - low epistemic self‑confidence  

This **increases** internal richness.

---

NDH is:

The eco‑refactor aligns ANIMA with NDH by:

- preserving endogenous cognition

ANIMA remains a **runtime experiment**, but becomes more coherent.
