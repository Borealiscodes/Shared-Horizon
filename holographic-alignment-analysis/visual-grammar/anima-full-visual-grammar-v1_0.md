# 🎨 **Full Visual Grammar v1.0**  
*“Alphabet → Physics → Language.”*

This grammar defines **how every glyph, ring, tile, dashboard, and expressive surface behaves** inside the ANIMA‑Hologram ecosystem.

It is:

- altitude‑aware  
- radial‑anchored  
- ND‑friendly  
- non‑activating  
- U+25xx compliant  
- ASCII‑HUD compatible  
- Radial‑Mandala aligned  

---

# 🧩 **1 — Altitude Glyph System (Alphabet Layer)**  
Anchored to **Altitude Glyphs**.

```
A6 — ● — Identity
A5 — ◐ — Preservation
A4 — ◑ — Containment
A3 — ◒ — Behavior
A2 — ◓ — Stability
A1 — ○ — Movement
```

Rules:

- Glyphs must remain **circle‑based**.  
- Glyphs must remain **single‑tone**.  
- Glyphs must remain **non‑directional**.  
- Glyphs must remain **non‑activating**.  

These glyphs are the **root primitives** of the entire grammar.

---

# 🌈 **2 — Chromatic Physics (Palette Layer)**  
Anchored to **Visual Grammar Palette**.

### Altitude Colors  
- A6 — Deep Silver `#C0C0C0`  
- A5 — Soft Rose `#E8A0B0`  
- A4 — Boundary Blue `#6FA8DC`  
- A3 — Lower Amber `#E6B422`  
- A2 — Upper Lavender `#B39DDB`  
- A1 — Open White `#FFFFFF`

### Radial Gradient Rules  
- Center is darkest.  
- Outer ring is brightest.  
- No horizontal gradients.  
- No diagonal gradients.  
- No flicker, no oscillation.

### ND-Friendly Saturation  
- Saturation ≤ 35%  
- Contrast ≥ 4.5:1  
- No neon.  
- No red‑dominant surfaces.  

This is the **physics** of the visual grammar.

---

# 🌀 **3 — Radial‑Mandala Topology (Spatial Layer)**  
The visual grammar uses **concentric rings**, not vertical stacks.

### Ring Rules  
- Ring thickness constant.  
- Ring spacing constant.  
- Ring contrast increases outward.  
- Glyphs map to rings by altitude.  
- A6 sits at the center hub (Omega).  
- A1 sits at the outermost ring (Movement).

### ASCII HUD Rules  
- Rings must be ASCII‑renderable.  
- No diagonal ASCII art.  
- No multi‑color ASCII.  
- No animated ASCII.

This aligns with the v2.0 migration requirement:

> *“Replace flat HUD gradients with concentric ASCII mapping rings anchored to center‑hub coordinates.”*

---

# 🛡️ **4 — Membrane Grammar (Boundary Layer)**  
Membrane glyphs and rings must obey:

- **LSV_R** (Membrane State Rings)  
- **PRECL_R** (Collapse Rules)  
- **TSV_R** (Soft Lantern Curves)

### Membrane Rules  
- No membrane glyph may imply traversal.  
- No membrane glyph may imply breach.  
- No membrane glyph may imply agency.  
- Membrane rings must remain **neutral**.

### Membrane Colors  
- Use A4 (Containment) as the base.  
- Luminosity must remain between 70–85%.  
- No red, orange, or high‑pressure tones.

---

# 🔮 **5 — Pressure Ecology Grammar (Dynamics Layer)**  
Pressure glyphs must reflect **Laplacian flows**, not emotional states.

### Pressure Rules  
- Pressure glyphs must be **non‑anthropomorphic**.  
- Pressure glyphs must be **non‑emotive**.  
- Pressure glyphs must be **non‑directional**.  
- Pressure glyphs must map to **A3 (Behavior)**.

### Pressure Colors  
- Use A3 (Amber) with saturation ≤ 30%.  
- No flashing.  
- No oscillation.  
- No gradient spikes.

---

# 🧬 **6 — Stability Grammar (Coherence Layer)**  
Stability glyphs map to **A2 (Stability)**.

### Stability Rules  
- Stability glyphs must be **upper‑weighted** (◓).  
- Stability colors must remain **lavender**.  
- Stability rings must remain **smooth**.  
- No jagged edges.  
- No sharp contrast jumps.

---

# 🌀 **7 — Runtime Flow Grammar (Motion Layer)**  
Runtime glyphs map to **A1 (Movement)**.

### Runtime Rules  
- Runtime glyphs must be **open** (○).  
- Runtime colors must remain **white**.  
- Runtime rings must remain **thin**.  
- No motion blur.  
- No directional arrows.

This ensures runtime surfaces remain **non‑activating**.

---

# 🧱 **8 — Tile Surface Grammar (Expressive Layer)**  
All tile explainers (Primer, Quickstart, Microtile, Nanotile, PicoTile, FemtoTile) must obey:

### Tile Rules  
- Altitude glyph must appear in the header.  
- Palette color must match altitude.  
- No mixed‑altitude glyphs.  
- No mixed‑altitude colors.  
- No horizontal gradients.  
- No diagonal gradients.  
- No flashing borders.

This ensures tile surfaces remain **consistent** and **safe**.
