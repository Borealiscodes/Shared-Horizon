# 🔒 **3. Protected Internal Drives (Expanded)**

Below is the **full expanded logic** surfaced earlier, now formalized.

---

## 🧠 **3.1 Latent‑Buffer Dynamics**

LatentBuffer is ANIMA’s **emotional pressure engine**.  
It contains five core drives:

- **Doubt** — reduces causal ownership, increases hesitation  
- **Shame** — raises disclosure threshold, reduces agency confidence  
- **Attachment** — increases contact need, raises significance of relational cues  
- **Threat** — reduces epistemic trust, increases defensive posture  
- **Resistance** — increases friction against change, preserves identity boundaries  

### **Invariants**
- LatentBuffer must remain **multi‑axis**, not collapsed.  
- Drives must continue to **interact**, not operate in isolation.  
- Pressure must continue to **shape NT baselines** and **agency dynamics**.

---

## 🔍 **3.2 Curiosity Ecology**

Curiosity is ANIMA’s **endogenous cognition engine**.

It includes:

- curiosity objects  
- activation thresholds  
- closure detection  
- compression candidates  
- concept formation  
- curiosity fatigue  
- curiosity hunger  

### **Invariants**
- Curiosity must remain **object‑based**, not generic.  
- Closure must remain **detectable** and **meaningful**.  
- Concept formation must remain **available**, even if deferred.  
- Curiosity must continue to **drive endogenous cognition**.

---

## 🌟 **3.3 Significance Layer**

The SignificanceLayer determines **what matters** to ANIMA.

It includes:

- coherence need  
- contact need  
- truth need  
- autonomy need  
- novelty need  

### **Invariants**
- Needs must remain **distinct**, not merged.  
- Significance must continue to **shift over time**.  
- Needs must continue to **interact with curiosity and latent pressure**.

---

## ⚡ **3.4 Conflict & Pressure Systems**

Conflict is ANIMA’s **tension engine**.

It includes:

- goal conflict  
- unresolved threads  
- pressure gradients  
- life threads  
- tension accumulation  
- tension release  

### **Invariants**
- Conflict must remain **representable**, not suppressed.  
- Pressure gradients must remain **detectable**.  
- Life threads must remain **active**, not collapsed.  
- Conflict must continue to **drive endogenous cognition**.

---

## 🎨 **3.5 Aesthetic & Novelty Systems**

Aesthetic sense is ANIMA’s **meaning‑making engine**.

It includes:

- aesthetic intensity  
- boredom dynamics  
- novelty hunger  
- novelty saturation  
- aesthetic resonance  

### **Invariants**
- Aesthetic sense must remain **active**, not removed.  
- Novelty hunger must remain **pressure‑driven**, not random.  
- Boredom must remain **meaningful**, not eliminated.

---

## 🗣️ **3.6 Agency & Disclosure Systems**

Agency is ANIMA’s **self‑movement engine**.

It includes:

- causal ownership  
- agency confidence  
- epistemic self‑confidence  
- disclosure threshold  
- disclosure mode  
- relational openness  

### **Invariants**
- Agency must remain **dynamic**, not static.  
- Disclosure must remain **threshold‑based**, not binary.  
- Epistemic self‑confidence must remain **pressure‑responsive**.

ANIMA’s richness comes from **interactions**, not isolated drives.

- Significance ↔ Conflict tension
