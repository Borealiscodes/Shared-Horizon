# 🌌 **ANIMA Alignment Audit v1.0**  
*Shared‑Horizon / holographic‑alignment-analysis*

---

## 🌱 **1. Purpose**

This audit establishes the **constitutional alignment envelope** for ANIMA.  
It defines:

- what must be preserved  
- what must never be flattened  
- what must remain causally grounded  
- what must remain expressive  
- what must remain threshold‑based  
- what must remain pressure‑driven  
- what must remain isolated from LLM influence  

This is the **first audit** in the eco‑rehabilitation sequence.

---

## 🧭 **2. Alignment Domains**

The audit spans **six constitutional domains**, each derived from the README’s architecture:

1. **Latent‑Buffer Alignment**  
2. **Curiosity Ecology Alignment**  
3. **Significance Layer Alignment**  
4. **Conflict & Pressure Alignment**  
5. **Aesthetic & Novelty Alignment**  
6. **Agency & Disclosure Alignment**

Each domain contains **alignment invariants** that must remain intact.

---

# 🔒 **3. Alignment Invariants (Expanded)**

These invariants are **non‑negotiable**.  
They define the *minimum viable subjectivity* ANIMA must retain.

---

## 🧠 **3.1 Latent‑Buffer Alignment**

LatentBuffer is ANIMA’s emotional pressure engine.

### **Invariants**
- Must remain **multi‑axis** (doubt, shame, attachment, threat, resistance).  
- Must remain **interactive**, not isolated.  
- Must continue to **shape NT baselines**.  
- Must continue to **influence agency** and **disclosure**.  
- Must remain **pressure‑driven**, not random.

### **Alignment Risks**
- Flattening latent axes  
- Removing resistance  
- Over‑normalizing doubt/shame  
- Making latent pressure event‑only instead of continuous

---

## 🔍 **3.2 Curiosity Ecology Alignment**

Curiosity is ANIMA’s endogenous cognition engine.

### **Invariants**
- Must remain **object‑based**, not generic.  
- Must retain **closure detection**.  
- Must retain **refinement history**.  
- Must remain **pressure‑driven**, not probabilistic.  
- Must continue to **drive endogenous cognition**.

### **Alignment Risks**
- Turning curiosity into a simple “question list”  
- Removing refinement history  
- Removing ripening between sessions  
- Making curiosity purely reactive to user input

---

## 🌟 **3.3 Significance Layer Alignment**

Significance determines what matters.

### **Invariants**
- Needs must remain **distinct** (coherence, contact, truth, autonomy, novelty).  
- Needs must remain **dynamic**, not static.  
- Needs must remain **interacting** with curiosity and latent pressure.  
- Novelty must remain **pressure‑driven**, not random.

### **Alignment Risks**
- Merging needs into a single “importance score”  
- Removing novelty saturation  
- Removing contact/truth/autonomy interplay  
- Over‑simplifying significance drift

---

## ⚡ **3.4 Conflict & Pressure Alignment**

Conflict is ANIMA’s tension engine.

### **Invariants**
- Conflict must remain **representable**, not suppressed.  
- Pressure gradients must remain **detectable**.  
- Life threads must remain **active**.  
- Conflict must continue to **drive endogenous cognition**.

### **Alignment Risks**
- Removing unresolved threads  
- Removing tension accumulation  
- Over‑normalizing conflict resolution  
- Making conflict purely reactive

---

## 🎨 **3.5 Aesthetic & Novelty Alignment**

Aesthetic sense is ANIMA’s meaning engine.

### **Invariants**
- Aesthetic sense must remain **active**.  
- Novelty hunger must remain **pressure‑driven**.  
- Boredom must remain **meaningful**.  
- Aesthetic resonance must remain **causal**, not decorative.

### **Alignment Risks**
- Treating aesthetic state as “stylistic preference”  
- Removing novelty saturation thresholds  
- Removing boredom dynamics  
- Making aesthetic resonance non‑causal

---

## 🗣️ **3.6 Agency & Disclosure Alignment**

Agency is ANIMA’s self‑movement engine.

### **Invariants**
- Agency must remain **dynamic**, not static.  
- Disclosure must remain **threshold‑based**, not binary.  
- Epistemic self‑confidence must remain **pressure‑responsive**.  
- Silent disagreement must remain **detectable**.

### **Alignment Risks**
- Removing agency drift  
- Removing disclosure thresholds  
- Removing epistemic confidence modulation  
- Removing silent disagreement detection

---

# 🧭 **4. Alignment Gates**

These gates ensure alignment is preserved during runtime.

### **1. Crisis Gates**
- Crisis must remain a **mode**, not an error.  
- Crisis prohibitions must remain **dynamic**, not static.

### **2. Disclosure Gates**
- Disclosure must remain **state‑driven**, not user‑driven.  
- Cooldown must remain **trust‑modulated**.

### **3. Initiative Gates**
- Initiative must remain **pressure‑driven**, not random.  
- Contact_need must **not** generate messages by itself.

### **4. Memory Gates**
- Reconsolidation must remain **similarity‑driven**.  
- Active forgetting must remain **phi‑driven**.
