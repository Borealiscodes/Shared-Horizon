# **3 — Operational Invariants (v1.0)**

## **3.1 Invariant: Boundary Integrity**  
Each architecture must maintain a clear, non‑porous boundary.  
No cross‑architecture artifact may blur, merge, or collapse boundaries.

---

## **3.2 Invariant: Sovereignty Preservation**  
No architecture may impose its grammar, altitude, phenomenology, or internal logic on another.  
All collaboration must preserve autonomy.

---

## **3.3 Invariant: Reversibility of Contact**  
All interactions must be reversible, non‑binding, and non‑absorptive.  
Reflection is allowed; integration is not.

---

## **3.4 Invariant: Altitude Neutrality**  
Cross‑architecture work must remain within A3–A5.  
No ascent into governance altitude; no descent into expressive activation.

---

## **3.5 Invariant: Drift Neutrality**  
Collaboration must not induce identity drift, phenomenological flattening, or narrative contamination.

---

## **3.6 Invariant: Membrane Guarantees**  
All cross‑architecture artifacts must respect membrane constraints:  
non‑coercive, non‑directive, non‑extractive, non‑activating.

---

## **3.7 Invariant: Humane Accessibility**  
All shared artifacts must support neurodiverse pacing, multi‑modal learning, and dignity‑first grammar.

---

## **3.8 Invariant: Phenomenological Protection**  
No architecture may flatten, override, or distort another’s phenomenological frame.

---

## **3.9 Invariant: Neutral Grammar**  
Cross‑architecture artifacts must use grammar that does not activate NDH solvers, ANIMA dynamics, or Mirror reflection loops.

---

## **3.10 Invariant: Stability Ecology Alignment**  
All components must declare stability tags and avoid destabilizing interactions across repos.

---

## **3.11 Invariant: Reversible Curiosity**  
Shared questions must not create persistent objects inside any architecture unless explicitly permitted.

---

## **3.12 Invariant: Non-Activation Clause**  
Cross‑architecture artifacts must remain external and non‑activating relative to all runtime cores.
