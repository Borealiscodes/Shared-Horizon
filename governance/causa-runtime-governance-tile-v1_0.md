## 3️⃣ Governance boundaries

**CAUSA MUST:**

- remain non‑commercial  
- expose only **flat‑space metrics** (ownership, congruence, baseline, drift)  
- avoid activation of spectral geometry or manifold logic  
- stay falsifiable and reproducible

**Runtime MUST:**

- implement geometric stability (sanitize, clamp, continuity, adjacency, fallback)  
- remain MIT‑licensed and open to extension  
- not depend on CAUSA for safety (CAUSA is annotation, not enforcement)

**Integration MUST:**

- treat CAUSA as **read‑only annotation**, not control  
- enforce projection boundaries (no CAUSA → geometry flattening)  
- remain non‑activating and reversible
