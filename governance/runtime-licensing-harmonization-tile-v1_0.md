## 1️⃣ Purpose

Define the licensing relationship between:

- **Spectral‑Base‑Runtime** (MIT‑licensed geometric stability engine)  
- **CAUSA** (non‑commercial evidentiary comparator)

so that:

- the runtime remains **open, adoptable, and extensible**  
- CAUSA remains **neutral, falsifiable, and non‑monetized**  
- integration stays **altitude‑safe, reversible, and non‑activating**

### **Spectral‑Base‑Runtime — MIT License**
- Encourages broad adoption across research, industry, and open‑source ecosystems  
- Allows modification, extension, and embedding without restriction  
- Preserves authorship and provenance  
- Ensures geometric stability logic remains accessible and widely usable  

### **CAUSA — Non‑Commercial**
- Protects evidentiary neutrality  
- Prevents profit‑driven bias in comparator metrics  
- Maintains reproducibility and peer‑review integrity  
- Ensures suitability for human‑rights, legal, and research contexts  

---

## 3️⃣ Harmonization Boundary

To maintain altitude safety:

- CAUSA **may annotate** runtime behavior  
- CAUSA **may not control** runtime behavior  
- Runtime **may ingest** CAUSA metrics  
- Runtime **may not depend** on CAUSA for safety  
- CAUSA metrics remain **flat‑space**, never geometric  
- Runtime geometry remains **MIT‑open**, never commercialized  

This boundary prevents collapse between:

- **Euclidean comparators**  
- **geometric stability operators**

---

## 4️⃣ Projection Constraints

- CAUSA → Runtime: **read‑only annotations**  
- Runtime → CAUSA: **observable deltas only**  
- No flattening of geometry into CAUSA vectors  
- No elevation of CAUSA metrics into geometric operators  
- All projections remain **non‑activating** and **reversible**
