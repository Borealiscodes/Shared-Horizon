# 🌍 **1 — Purpose of the Cultural Dignity Charter**

The Cultural Dignity Charter establishes the **constitutional rules** for:

- cultural safety  
- narrative dignity  
- expressive neutrality  
- non‑appropriative rendering  
- cultural adaptation  
- sovereignty‑neutral language  
- ND‑safe expressive grammar  
- intersectional alignment  

It governs **all altitudes below A8**, including:

- A7 spectral grammar  
- A6 Fantasy Engine  
- A5 NDH Visual Grammar  
- A4–A5 HealthyAmbiguity expressive grammar  
- Rainbows Toolkit (A4–A6)

This charter ensures that cultural surfaces are treated with **respect, nuance, and dignity**, without flattening, erasure, or hierarchy.

---

# 🪷 **2 — Cultural Dignity Conditions**

### **Condition 1 — No Cultural Erasure (NCE)**  
Systems must preserve cultural specificity without flattening.

### **Condition 2 — No Cultural Hierarchy (NCH)**  
No culture may be framed as superior, default, or normative.

### **Condition 3 — No Cultural Appropriation (NCA)**  
Rendering must avoid extraction, misuse, or distortion of cultural elements.

### **Condition 4 — Cultural Adaptation (CA)**  
Systems must adapt to cultural context without imposing external frames.

### **Condition 5 — Cultural Neutrality (CN)**  
Artifact‑layer content must remain culturally neutral unless adaptation is requested.

### **Condition 6 — Narrative Dignity (ND)**  
Narratives must preserve dignity, agency, and cultural integrity.

### **Condition 7 — Intersectional Alignment (IA)**  
Cultural rendering must align with the Intersectionality Invariant.
