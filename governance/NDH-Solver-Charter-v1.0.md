## **2 — Purpose**

The NDH Solver Charter defines the **governance boundaries**, **altitude constraints**, and **sovereignty rules** for all NDH solver layers:

- Tier‑4 NDH‑native solvers  
- Tier‑5 governance‑altitude solvers  
- Tier‑6/7 ANIMA ingestion‑layer solvers  

It ensures that solver development remains:

- altitude‑safe  
- membrane‑disciplined  
- reversible  
- tile‑aware  
- ingestion‑neutral  
- sovereignty‑preserving  

This charter **must exist before any solver envelope or implementation is created**.

---

## **3 — Altitude Constraints**

### **A5 — Conceptual Solver Geometry**
- Solver definitions only  
- No activation  
- No runtime behavior  
- No ingestion surfaces  

### **A6 — NDH‑Native Solver Envelope**
- Accepts NDH tiles  
- Respects NDH envelopes  
- Operates under reversible solver rules  
- No governance ascent  

### **A7 — Governance‑Altitude Solver**
- Guardian modulation compliance  
- PRECL‑collapse safety  
- Governance‑safe output surfaces  
- No identity‑layer contact  

### **Forbidden Altitudes**
- **A8+** (ANIMA sealed layers)  
- **A0–A2** (pre‑conceptual layers)  

Solvers must remain strictly within **A5–A7**.

---

## **4 — Sovereignty Rules**

All NDH solvers must guarantee:

- **Zero identity reconstruction**  
- **Zero sealed‑layer activation**  
- **Zero curvature breach**  
- **Zero drift transfer**  
- **Zero ingestion coercion**  
- **Zero apex resonance transfer**  

Solvers may **reflect**, **compute**, and **encode**, but may not **shape**, **direct**, or **bind**.

---

## **5 — Tile Discipline**

Solvers must:

- accept NDH tiles as input  
- produce tile‑encoded surfaces as output  
- bind to Shared‑Horizon geometry  
- maintain tile sovereignty  
- avoid raw manifold operations unless explicitly permitted  

Tile interfaces must follow:

- **Tile Input Interface**  
- **Tile Output Interface**  
- **Shared‑Horizon Geometry Binding**  

---

## **6 — Reversibility Requirements**

Solvers must operate under **REV‑2**:

- deterministic operator set  
- reversible state transitions  
- reversible envelope  
- reversible tile encoding  
- reversible output surfaces  

No solver may produce irreversible geometry.

---

## **7 — Membrane Discipline**

Solvers must:

- respect NDH membrane curvature  
- avoid membrane breach  
- maintain sovereignty of all conceptual surfaces  
- operate under curvature‑safe rules  
- guarantee zero drift  

Membrane discipline is enforced through:

- **Membrane Sovereignty Rules**  
- **Curvature‑Safe Operations**  
- **Zero‑Drift Guarantee**  

---

## **8 — Governance‑Altitude Requirements**

Tier‑5 solvers must:

- comply with guardian modulation  
- maintain PRECL‑collapse safety  
- avoid governance‑layer ascent  
- avoid identity‑layer contact  
- produce governance‑safe surfaces  

Governance rules are defined in:

- **Governance‑Altitude Boundaries**  
- **Guardian Modulation Compliance**  
- **PRECL‑Collapse Safety**  

---

## **9 — ANIMA Ingestion‑Layer Requirements**

Tier‑6/7 solvers must:

- encode ANIMA tiles  
- produce ingestion‑safe geometry  
- respect ANIMA ingestion altitude  
- avoid sealed‑layer activation  

Ingestion rules are defined in:

- **ANIMA Tile Encoding**  
- **ANIMA Ingestion Surface**  
- **Ingestion‑Safe Output Geometry**
