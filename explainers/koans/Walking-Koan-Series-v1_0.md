# 🌿 **Walking Koan Series v1.0**

## 1 — **The Path That Follows You**
> A traveler asked, “How do I find the right path?”  
> The teacher said, **“Walk until the path finds you.”**

Reflection: Orientation is reciprocal — you shape the world and the world shapes you.  
Explore more through **orientation**.

---

## 2 — **The Step That Isn’t Taken**
> A student said, “I hesitate.”  
> The teacher replied, **“Then let the hesitation walk first.”**

Reflection: Movement includes pauses; pauses are part of the geometry.  
Deepen through **stillness**.

---

## 3 — **The Wind’s Instruction**
> The walker asked, “What guides me?”  
> The wind answered, **“Only what you can feel.”**

Reflection: Sensation is a compass; perception is a map.  
Continue with **perception**.

---

## 4 — **The Street That Remains**
> A monk walked all day.  
> At dusk he said, **“The street never moved.”**

Reflection: Sometimes the world is the constant and you are the variable.  
Follow with **constancy**.

---

## 5 — **The Footprint That Teaches**
> A novice asked, “What should I study?”  
> The teacher said, **“Your footprints.”**

Reflection: You learn from the traces you leave behind.  
Expand through **self‑trace**.

---

## 6 — **The Door That Opens Outward**
> A wanderer said, “I seek freedom.”  
> The teacher replied, **“Then stop closing the door behind you.”**

Reflection: Freedom is often the absence of self‑imposed boundaries.  
Explore **freedom**.

---

## 7 — **The Sky That Doesn’t Answer**
> A student asked the sky a question.  
> The sky remained silent.  
> The student bowed.

Reflection: Not all questions require answers; some require acceptance.  
Continue with **acceptance**.

---

## 8 — **The Walk That Walks You**
> A monk said, “I am walking.”  
> The teacher replied, **“And the walk is walking you.”**

Reflection: Movement is mutual; orientation is relational.  
Deepen through **mutuality**.

---

## 9 — **The Turning That Isn’t a Turn**
> A traveler turned left.  
> The world turned with them.

Reflection: Direction is co‑constructed; geometry is participatory.  
Explore **direction**.

---

## 10 — **The Step That Ends the Question**
> A student asked, “What is the Way?”  
> The teacher said, **“You just took it.”**

Reflection: Inquiry and action are the same gesture.  
Continue with **the_Way**.
