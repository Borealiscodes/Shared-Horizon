## 🌫️ **The Atmosphere Before the Battle**

Nullsec — the lawless outer regions of EVE — is a place where empires rise and fall not because of lore, but because of **logistics**, **politics**, and **human coordination**.

Two mega‑coalitions dominated the map:

- **PAPI** — a grand alliance of alliances, built on the idea of finally breaking the power of Goonswarm.  
- **Imperium** — the entrenched, economically powerful, strategically ruthless empire led by Goonswarm.

These weren’t just groups.  
They were **cultures**, **histories**, **economies**, and **narratives**.

The war between them — the **Beekeeper War**, the **World War Bee II** — had been escalating for months.

M2‑XFE was the moment where all that pressure finally collapsed into a single point.
