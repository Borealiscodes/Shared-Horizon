# 🧩 **2 — Structural Comparison (Resolver‑Safe)**

### **A — Commercial Use**
- **MIT:** Fully allowed.  
- **ANIMA:** Strictly prohibited without a separate agreement.

### **B — Redistribution**
- **MIT:** Allowed, even commercially, with minimal requirements.  
- **ANIMA:** Allowed only non‑commercially, with mandatory attribution.

### **C — Derivative Works**
- **MIT:** Derivatives may be closed‑source, commercial, or proprietary.  
- **ANIMA:** Derivatives must remain non‑commercial and carry the same license.

### **D — Corporate Use**
- **MIT:** Corporations may use, sell, integrate, or modify freely.  
- **ANIMA:** Corporations must obtain a separate commercial license.

### **E — Warranty**
Both disclaim all warranties — identical in spirit.
