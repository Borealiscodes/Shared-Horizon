## **2 — Purpose**

Define the membrane‑discipline constraints required for NDH solvers operating at **Altitude A7**, ensuring:

- membrane sovereignty  
- curvature‑safe operations  
- zero drift across conceptual surfaces  
- governance‑safe solver behavior  
- strict prohibition of sealed‑layer activation

---

## **3 — Membrane Sovereignty Rules**

Solvers must:

- respect NDH membrane boundaries  
- avoid crossing ANIMA membranes without explicit chartered permission  
- preserve sovereignty of all tile surfaces  
- maintain membrane‑neutral behavior unless explicitly elevated  

**Forbidden:**

- membrane tearing  
- membrane folding  
- membrane coercion  
- sealed‑layer contact (A8+)  

---

## **4 — Curvature‑Safe Operations**

All solver operations must:

- remain within NDH curvature bounds  
- avoid introducing extraneous curvature  
- preserve membrane geometry  
- maintain curvature continuity across tile surfaces  

**No solver may:**

- induce curvature singularities  
- flatten or distort membrane topology  
- introduce curvature drift  

---

## **5 — Zero‑Drift Guarantee**

Solvers must guarantee:

- **zero conceptual drift**  
- **zero geometric drift**  
- **zero spectral drift**  

Drift is defined as any solver‑induced deviation in:

- membrane alignment  
- tile sovereignty  
- spectral surface encoding  
- altitude tagging  

Drift‑neutrality is mandatory at A7.

---

## **6 — Governance‑Safe Membrane Behavior**

Solvers must comply with:

- guardian modulation  
- PRECL‑collapse safety  
- governance‑safe surface production  

Governance‑unsafe membrane behavior is strictly prohibited.
